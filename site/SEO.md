# SEO strategy for iphoneduoanimation.com

## Product keyword focus — 12 September 2026

The homepage targets **Duo animation for Mac**, **iPhone Duo animation for Mac**,
**free Duo animation for Mac**, **open source Duo animation**, and **MacBook lid
animation**. Use the natural spelling "open source" in copy; do not stuff in
"opensource" variants or add a meta-keywords tag.

- Homepage: aligned title, H1, description, social cards, visible FAQs and source-download copy.
- /blog/duo-animation-for-mac/: a setup guide for download, installation,
  compatibility and troubleshooting intent, linked from the homepage, blog hub
  and lid-sensor article and included in sitemap.xml.
- Structured data: connected WebSite, WebPage, SoftwareApplication and
  SoftwareSourceCode entities; the guide has TechArticle and BreadcrumbList data.
  The price is zero and source-build/prototype requirements are explicit. No
  invented reviews, ratings or promise of rich results.
- Canonical origin: https://www.iphoneduoanimation.com. The non-www origin
  returned a 308 redirect to www on 12 September 2026; www returned 200 from
  Vercel. Canonicals, structured-data URLs, social URLs and the sitemap now use
  the final origin. The existing Sites registration is unpublished and private;
  the public SEO target is the existing Vercel site.

These are relevance targets based on the requested phrases, not newly measured
search volumes or ranking forecasts. After publication, submit
https://www.iphoneduoanimation.com/sitemap.xml in Search Console and inspect the
homepage and setup-guide URLs. Track impressions, clicks and positions for this
cluster before expanding it. Deployment and Search Console submission are
separate actions; local edits do not establish that either has happened.

References: [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials),
[title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), and
[snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).

## 1. The honest ceiling

The brief was "traffic in millions". That is not reachable from this keyword space,
and it is better to say so now than to discover it in six months.

The only million-scale terms adjacent to this product are phone terms:

| Keyword | Volume/mo | KD | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| iphone fold | 165,000 | 4 | **Do not target** |
| foldable iphone | 135,000 | 25 | **Do not target** |
| apple iphone fold | 27,100 | 17 | **Do not target** |
| iphone flip | 12,100 | 26 | **Do not target** |

The difficulty is genuinely low and the domain name is suggestive, so ranking is not
impossible. It is still the wrong move. Everyone searching those terms wants news
about a folding *phone*. They do not want a Mac app. They would land, see a MacBook,
and leave — and a page that collects clicks and immediate bounces teaches Google the
page is a poor result, which drags down the terms you *can* win.

**Realistic ceiling for the relevant clusters below: roughly 25,000–40,000 searches
per month of addressable volume.** At strong-but-achievable positions that is on the
order of **3,000–8,000 organic visits a month within 6–12 months**, from a standing
start on a new site with no backlink profile. For a free open source utility that is
a good outcome. It is not millions.

If millions is the actual goal, SEO is the wrong channel for it. Launch traffic for
tools like this comes from Hacker News, Reddit (r/macapps, r/apple), Product Hunt and
X — spikes of tens of thousands in a day. SEO is what keeps a baseline afterwards.

## 3. Deliberately not targeted

- **Wallpaper terms.** "4k mac wallpapers", "macbook wallpaper" and friends are
  8,000–9,900/mo each at KD 21–30. Huge, but the intent is *download an image*. We do
  not have a wallpaper library and building one to chase this would be a different
  product.
- **Battery terms.** "macbook battery" and relatives surfaced in the related-keyword
  pull at 1,000–5,400/mo. Entirely unrelated to what this is.
- **The foldable-phone terms** in section 1.

## 4. What was built

```
/blog/                                        hub
/blog/macbook-lid-angle-sensor/               cluster E (authority + links)
/blog/macbook-clamshell-mode/                 cluster D
/blog/stop-macbook-sleeping-lid-closed/       cluster C
/blog/best-free-mac-screensavers/             cluster A
/blog/minimal-clock-screensaver-mac/          cluster B
/blog/screencapturekit-single-screenshot/     cluster F
```

Per page: unique title and meta description, canonical, OG and Twitter cards,
`Article`/`TechArticle`/`HowTo` schema, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` where there
are real questions. All JSON-LD validated as parseable. Sitemap updated. Internal
links run hub → article → homepage and article → article across clusters.

## 5. What actually moves the needle next

Ranked by effect, not by effort:

1. **Links.** The site has effectively no backlink profile, and that — not on-page
   work — is the binding constraint. A Hacker News or r/macapps post that does well
   earns more authority in a day than months of content. The lid-sensor article is
   the best link bait here.
2. **Verify in Search Console.** None of this is measurable until the property is
   verified and the sitemap submitted. Do this first; it costs ten minutes.
3. **Ship the app properly.** A notarised download instead of "build it yourself"
   would unlock "free mac apps" (390/mo, KD 44) and the download-intent terms, which
   currently have nothing to point at.
4. **Real screenshots.** Every page shares one OG image. Per-article images would
   improve click-through from search and make the pages shareable.
5. **Only then, more articles.** Extend clusters that show impressions in Search
   Console rather than guessing at new ones.
